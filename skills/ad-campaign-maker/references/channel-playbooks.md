# Channel playbooks

Use this when adapting ads to a channel or platform.

## Universal adaptation rules

- Match user intent: interruption channels need stronger hooks; search channels need intent matching; retargeting can assume more awareness.
- Fit the native behavior of the platform: do not make every ad sound like a TV commercial.
- Adjust CTA by funnel stage: awareness = learn/watch/follow; consideration = compare/get guide/book demo; conversion = buy/claim/start.
- Produce variants by changing the angle, not just swapping adjectives.

## Short video platforms

Useful for TikTok, Douyin, Reels, Shorts, Kuaishou, Video Channels.

- Start with a visual or verbal hook in 1-3 seconds.
- Use one of these hook types: pain-point callout, surprising result, myth-busting, before/after, challenge, POV, testimonial, demo-first.
- Keep scenes short and visible even without sound.
- Include captions/on-screen text.
- End with a simple CTA and a reason to act now.

## Xiaohongshu / lifestyle social

- Prefer authentic experience, recommendation, checklist, comparison, diary, or “避坑/攻略” framing.
- Avoid overly hard-sell language unless the user requests strong promotion.
- Include title options, cover text, body copy, and comment-pinned CTA when relevant.

## Ecommerce product ad

- Lead with problem, desired outcome, or offer.
- Show product mechanism and usage scenario.
- Include objections: price, quality, shipping, fit, trust, after-sales.
- Make variants for different audiences or use cases.

## B2B lead-generation ad

- Target by role, pain, cost of inaction, and measurable business outcome.
- Offer a low-friction next step: report, demo, consultation, calculator, checklist.
- Use proof placeholders if case data is not provided.
- Avoid fluffy claims; prioritize specificity and credibility.

## Search ads

- Mirror the keyword intent in headlines.
- Use offer/proof/CTA in descriptions.
- Keep each asset modular so the platform can combine them.
- Ask for character limits if the platform or locale requires strict compliance.

## Outdoor/print/display banner

- One message only.
- Large readable headline.
- Short CTA or URL/QR cue.
- Visual hierarchy: product/benefit first, proof second, CTA third.
